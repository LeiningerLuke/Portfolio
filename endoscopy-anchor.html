02 · Design

How the Concepts Differ

Design 1: compact base

A two-layer 20 mm square base with a T-handle pin rising from the top. It's a short, stable footprint built from six simple bodies.

Design 2: slim plunger

A 12 mm-wide, 81 mm-long body. A disc-topped plunger runs through a cylindrical housing and down a narrow shaft to a small multi-prong grip at the tip.

Both concepts use simple, printable geometry to keep cost down, which is the main design constraint for low-resource settings.
